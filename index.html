Agents with partial knowledge

Planner-guided agents acquire hidden capabilities and reuse them across budget-constrained tasks.

4.5–7.8×fewer contacts with persistent memory
62% feweraction schemas disclosed vs. Reveal-All
Method & findings +

Symbolic diagnosis identifies missing capabilities, and an LLM ranks providers from public profiles. Selective disclosure expands a partial action model; persistent memory reuses discoveries across tasks. Planner-guided acquisition disclosed 62% fewer action schemas than Reveal-All, memory reuse reduced contacts by 4.5–7.8×, and all 576 single-task trials produced independently validated plans.

Gomoku through self-play

AlphaZero-style self-play with pruning-aware MCTS.

60%best recorded win rate vs. pure MCTS
6.9×faster training: ~48 → 7 hours
Method & findings +

An AlphaZero-style policy–value network guides self-play and MCTS. Local candidate restriction narrows the search, while threat-aware exceptions preserve immediate winning and blocking moves. Training on 8×8 connect-5 fell from about 48 to 7 hours (6.9× faster), with a 60% best recorded win rate against pure MCTS.

Training-free visual grounding

YOLOv8n + OpenCLIP + spatial reasoning. No task-specific training.

0.15 Btotal model parameters
62.8%Acc@0.5 on RefCOCO-M
Method & findings +

YOLOv8n region proposals, OpenCLIP crop–text matching, and explicit spatial reasoning form a training-free, 0.15B-parameter system. Each prediction includes an explanation of its score. On 5,598 RefCOCO-M queries, the full pipeline reached 62.8% Acc@0.5 and 60.9% Acc@0.7; spatial reasoning alone improved Acc@0.5 by 16 percentage points over the CLIP-only baseline.
